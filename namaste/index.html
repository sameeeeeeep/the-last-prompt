What is The Last Prompt?

We are building the intelligent layer between humans and reality. Our thesis is simple: the prompt is the last bottleneck between people and superintelligence. Not the model. Not the compute. The friction of knowing what to ask, how to ask it, and when.

The Last Prompt exists to eliminate that friction — so that one day, no human ever has to write a prompt again.

Our architecture: ARIA

Agentic Reality Interface Architecture. An intelligence layer that wraps around a person's entire digital and real life. It sees what you see, hears what you hear, and acts before you ask.

Phase 1 — Screen (Live)

Ambient awareness of the user's screen, active apps, and workflow state. Currently shipping as Autoclaw, an open-source agentic interface layer for macOS.

Phase 2 — Voice (Soon)

Conversational intelligence that listens, understands context, and offers the right thought at the right time — not as a command interface, but as a calmer presence.

Phase 3 — Vision (Soon)

Real-world perception. ARIA sees the physical environment and surfaces relevant intelligence without being asked.

Phase 4 — Neural (Unplanned)

The interface disappears entirely. No prompt. No screen. You just know.

Our product: Autoclaw

Autoclaw is the first embodiment of ARIA — an open-source agentic interface layer for macOS. It collapses the cognitive load between humans and AI tools.

  • Watches your screen and understands your workflow context
  • Crafts prompts for you — so you never have to figure out what to type
  • Plans your next action before you finish the current one
  • Monitors your sessions, maintains your todos, keeps you in flow
  • Open-source — built in public at github.com/sameeeeeeep/autoclaw
