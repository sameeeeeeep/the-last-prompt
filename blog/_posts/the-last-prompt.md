Every few months a model crosses a line we said it wouldn’t. It writes the code, drafts the contract, reads the scan, argues the case. On the benchmark, it is already superhuman.

And yet the felt experience of using it has barely moved: you sit at a blank box and explain yourself, again, from scratch. The intelligence is extraordinary. The interface to it is a text field.

We think that gap, between how capable the model is and how little of that capability reaches an ordinary person on an ordinary day, is the most important problem in applied AI. It is not a modelling problem. It is an architecture problem.

The Last Prompt is a lab built to solve it.

## The bar

You don’t prompt a good colleague through every step; you share a goal and they run. That is the bar.

Superintelligence lands the moment it becomes intuitive to use, not the moment it clears a benchmark. Closing that gap is the whole job.

> The last prompt is the one you don’t have to write.
