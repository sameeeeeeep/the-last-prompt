Look at a real prompt. Not a demo, an actual one you’d send during work. Most of it isn’t the question. It’s setup.

> You’re a growth marketer. We sell ceramic cookware, tone is plain. I already tried a discount push, it flopped. Here’s the spend file again. Never use emojis.

Five lines of context to earn one line of actual work: *where did the money go?*

You wrote all five before. The model has been told all five before. Nothing carried over, so you pay the tax again.

Multiply that by every conversation, every tool, every day, and the prompt stops looking like an input and starts looking like what it is: the seam where a stateless intelligence meets a stateful life.

You are the integration layer. You are the memory. You are the thing holding it all in your head.
