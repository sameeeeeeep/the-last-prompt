There is a path, and it has stages.

**Prompted** is where we are now: you carry everything to a blank box.

**Primed** is the near future we’re building: the system already holds your context, your files, your standing intentions, so a nudge is enough. The work starts closer to done.

**Promptless** is the destination: the system sees the situation, understands what it takes, and acts before you have to assemble a request at all.

Promptless is not magic and it is not autonomy for its own sake. It’s the ordinary endpoint of an interface that finally has enough context to stop asking.

You don’t prompt a good colleague through every step; you share a goal and they run. That is the bar.

If you want to find out which stage you are at, and move one up in a day, that is what the [workshops](/workshops/) are for.
